Ideas Lab · Prototype

Write your proposal

This form prepares a public GitHub issue. It does not send your idea to DNCH until you review and submit it on GitHub.

6–100 characters
20–3000 characters. Do not include passwords, wallet recovery phrases or sensitive personal information.

How publishing works

GitHub will open with your title, topic and description prefilled. Sign in, review your text and press Submit new issue there. Nothing is posted automatically.

Community principles

  1. Be constructive and respectful
  2. Credit creators and respect copyrights
  3. No harassment, illegal content, scams or spam
  4. Do not publish private information
  5. All suggestions are reviewed; approval is not guaranteed
