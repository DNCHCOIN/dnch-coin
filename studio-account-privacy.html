DNCH ID & MAIL / PRIVACY

Your account and messages.

DNCH Studio stores your chosen username, display name, account creation time, password authentication data, a hash of your recovery code, internal messages, read status and drafts. Your internal address is your username followed by @dnch. It is not an external email address.

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth. The recovery code is shown once; only its hash is saved. Save your code privately, because there is no external email reset for this account.

Messages are accessible through the service to the sender and recipient. Drafts are accessible only to their owner. This service does not provide end-to-end encryption; authorized infrastructure administrators can access stored records when operating or supporting the service.

Account and message data are stored in the existing Supabase project in the EU West region. Your browser keeps sign-in tokens in session storage so you stay signed in in that browser tab. Signing out clears that tab's saved session.

We use short-lived rate counters based on a hash of the request IP address and your account ID to limit automated registrations, login attempts and message sending. Expired rate counters are cleaned up as the service is used.

Deleting a message hides your copy. The other participant retains their copy, and the underlying record remains stored. Draft deletion removes the draft. To request account or data deletion, use the DNCH Studio contact form and identify your internal username. Account verification is required.

Do not send sensitive documents or information through internal mail. This first version supports text messages; attachments are not enabled.
