Updated October 2026 · Product strategy

DNCH Development Roadmap

Build useful products, test demand, and invest effort where real users find value. Statuses describe progress, not launch promises.

Live pilots · Improving

01 · Hub & Markets

Improve DNCH Hub, DNCH Today and the public Markets pilot: prices, interactive charts, historical views, candlesticks and mobile usability. Validate reliability and data-provider limits.

Pilot / Development

02 · Community & Identity

Develop forum discussions, Ideas Lab, user profiles and moderation. Test useful daily challenges and cross-product missions without encouraging spam.

Concept · Next prototype

03 · DNCH MIND

One logic-game platform with two connected worlds: Logic Lab (circuits and puzzles) and Mystery Files (detective cases). Start with browser/mobile prototypes, then assess Android and iOS releases.

Pilot / Exploring

04 · Radio, Games & Daily Utility

Improve the Radio YouTube-player pilot and Drag Racing prototype; explore more everyday tools, educational content and accessible entertainment. Licensed 24/7 streaming remains a separate future step.

Planned · Validation first

05 · Rewards & Retention

Explore optional Hub missions, crosswords, quizzes and verified contributions that unlock in-game hints or cosmetics. Game points remain separate from blockchain tokens and have no cash value.

Long-term · Conditional

06 · Sustainable Business & Web3

Measure adoption, retention, maintenance costs and revenue before scaling. Evaluate optional ads, partnerships, premium features, portfolios and compliant token utility only when justified.

Our operating principle: user value first, measurable experiments second, monetization only where sustainable. No guaranteed dates, financial returns or token price.
