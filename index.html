The idea

People first. Utility first.

DNCH began as a community-driven token project on Solana. Our broader ambition is a welcoming digital ecosystem where people discover music, share ideas, explore useful content, and play — whether or not they own crypto.

DNCH Ideas Lab — proposed workflow

  1. Share your idea
  2. Discuss and improve it together
  3. Review feasibility and safety
  4. Publish decisions and progress

Ideas are welcome; submission does not guarantee implementation or rewards.

Build in phases

A realistic path forward.

We will launch useful pieces one by one, listen to early users and grow only when each foundation works.

Foundation

Audit the token and website, document decisions and establish a reliable project status.

Community & content

Publish the hub, share updates and prepare a forum and editorial workflow.

Radio & mobile

Validate the audience, licenses, streaming stack and Android/iOS experience.

Games & Web3

Prototype Drag Racing, useful AI features and any legally viable token integrations.
