Our story

VECTOR AUTO is a fictional auto repair shop created for the DNCH Digital Studio portfolio. Its sample backstory follows a family garage growing into a multidisciplinary diagnostics, repair and restoration center. The goal is to demonstrate how a real workshop could present its capabilities, equipment and people online.

  • Diagnose before repairing
  • Clear scope and indicative quotes
  • Care for every vehicle

Services and equipment

01

Engines and transmissions

Gasoline and diesel engine repair, timing systems, turbochargers, head gaskets, clutches, manual and automatic transmissions.

02

Computer diagnostics

OBD scans, fault codes, live data, sensors, electronics, batteries, alternators and starters.

03

Wheel alignment and suspension

Front and rear axle alignment, geometry checks, dampers, joints, bearings and steering systems.

04

Brakes and safety

Discs, pads, calipers, brake fluid, ABS diagnostics and safety checks.

05

Bodywork and panel beating

Repairing minor collision damage, panel straightening, body preparation and replacement panels.

06

Paint and detailing

Surface preparation, priming, panel painting, polishing and cosmetic paint correction.

07

Air conditioning and cooling

Air conditioning diagnostics, leak checks, radiators, water pumps, thermostats and cooling systems.

08

Tires and routine maintenance

Tire fitting and balancing, pressure checks, seasonal inspections, oil and filters.

Parts and consumables
