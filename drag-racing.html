HOW TO PLAY

Side-view engine torque, clutch and traction simulation. Try RWD, FWD or AWD and adjust launch throttle.

Tap START RACE and wait for the green light. Then hold GAS to accelerate.

Watch RPM and shift close to the redline, before the rev limiter.

Desktop: → or W for gas, Space / ↑ to shift, Enter to start.

Earn fictional game credits after a completed race and spend them on engine, traction and gearbox upgrades. No purchases or blockchain payments.

All garage credits, upgrades and personal bests are stored only in this browser. Clearing browser storage erases them. No online ranking or cross-device sync yet.
