# DNCH Community — Draft Principles

*Working proposal; not a published platform policy.*

## Who it's for

People from anywhere who want to discuss music, games, technology, creativity, life, learning, crypto and ideas beyond DNCH. Owning DNCH is not required.

## Participation

- Be respectful, constructive and welcoming to different cultures and viewpoints.
- Share topics and proposals clearly; write what you want to achieve, why it matters and what help you need.
- Critique ideas rather than people. No threats, discrimination, harassment, spam, scams, impersonation or deceptive promotion.
- Do not share passwords, wallet recovery phrases, private keys or other sensitive personal information.
- Respect creators, licenses and copyrighted works; do not post infringing material.
- No claims of guaranteed returns, promised token value or guaranteed contribution rewards.
- Forum moderation decisions should be explainable and allow appropriate appeals.

## DNCH Ideas Lab proposal workflow

1. **Proposed:** a member submits a suggestion, possibly about topics outside DNCH.
2. **Discussion:** participants assess usefulness, accessibility and technical feasibility.
3. **Review:** moderators and maintainers consider safety, legal and budget constraints.
4. **Planned / Not planned:** publish a decision with reasons when possible.
5. **In progress / Shipped:** track implemented ideas transparently.

These labels represent **future workflow stages**, not active infrastructure. No automatic rewards, tokens or development commitments follow a submission.

## Moderation and privacy (before production)

A production forum needs privacy notices, terms, reporting, rate limiting, spam filters, age and content policies, an abuse/escalation process and moderation tooling. AI assistance may support translations and duplicate detection but should not replace human review of significant decisions.

## Current prototype

Ideas Lab currently leads to **public GitHub Issues**. GitHub collects information under its own terms/privacy policy. Review the issue text before posting; contributions are public. A full DNCH forum and its account system remain planned, not live.
