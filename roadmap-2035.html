Next 90-day priorities

Publish the mission and roadmap; prototype worldwide DNCH GUIDE; integrate AI with verifiable sources; improve Studio and serve first clients; gather community ideas; publish costs and limitations.

How we measure success

Useful searches and routes, real clients, active contributors, implemented ideas, verified merchants and sustainable revenue relative to operating costs.

Important: This is a long-term vision, not a delivery guarantee. Features depend on resources, security, regulation and real demand. Planned services and DNCH payments are not represented as already operational.
