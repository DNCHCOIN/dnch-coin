Why DNCH exists

More than another token.

DNCH began with DNCH Coin and the technology around it. But a token on its own isn't a reason to come back every day. That led to a bigger idea: DNCH Hub — a place for music, knowledge, community, creativity and games, open even to visitors who never use a crypto wallet.

The journey

A small start. A bigger vision.

01

The beginning

An idea and a symbolic €3. Not a promise to get rich — a decision to start building from scratch.

02

DNCH Coin

The token gave the project a technical starting point and a name. The next question: what would be genuinely useful to people?

03

DNCH Hub

A vision emerged for one connected platform serving many interests instead of focusing only on cryptocurrency.

04

What's next

Community, radio, practical daily content, games and AI tools — ideas being developed in stages, not services we claim are already live.

What we stand for

Principles for building DNCH

✦

People before the token

DNCH should offer something to every visitor, including people who never use cryptocurrency.

◈

Open to ideas

People from different countries should be able to propose topics, join conversations and help shape the experience.

◎

Progress without hype

We distinguish live features from plans. We do not promise profits, returns or any future token price.

Earn by Building

This story does not have a final chapter.

Our aim is to develop DNCH with the people who use it. For now, suggestions go through the GitHub-based Ideas Lab prototype; a real forum and user accounts are still on the roadmap.
