Privacy notice for project inquiries

This notice describes how DNCH Digital Studio handles information that you choose to provide when requesting a quote or discussing a digital project through this website.

What we collect

We request your name or company name, email address, chosen service and project details. You may also provide a phone number and indicative budget. Please do not submit passwords, payment card details, private keys or sensitive personal information.

Why we use it

We use your inquiry details to review your request, contact you about it and, if appropriate, prepare a proposed scope and quotation. We do not ask you to create an account.

Email delivery service

Our website sends form submissions to FormSubmit, a third-party service that forwards them to our contact email inbox. FormSubmit may process and temporarily retain submission information to operate its service. You can read the provider's website and documentation for further details. The form may also show CAPTCHA verification to prevent spam.

How to contact us

If you want to ask about an inquiry, request correction or deletion of the information you sent, or discuss how your details are used, contact killkiro47@gmail.com.

Other considerations

We use HTTPS for the website and form destination. Sending an inquiry does not create a contract. We may revise this notice if the form delivery service or data handling changes.
