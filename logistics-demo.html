ABOUT THE CONCEPT

From the first pallet to the last mile

NORTHLINE LOGISTICS is a fictional freight company built as a portfolio demo. It illustrates regional distribution, consolidated shipments, pallet transport and route planning. The goal is to show customers what can be moved and how an indicative quote works.

This story is illustrative and does not describe an operating company.
