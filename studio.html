Frequently asked questions

Before we get started.

How much does a website cost?

Basic business websites start at approximately €100. Final price and schedule depend on pages, features and content.

Can we add features later?

Yes. We can build a core version first, then add modules such as catalogs, forms, calculators and integrations.

Are domain and hosting included?

Not by default. Domains, paid hosting, external API services and maintenance are quoted separately.

Can we explore actual demos?

Yes. Open the four interactive concepts above. They showcase approaches and features, but are not commissioned client projects.
