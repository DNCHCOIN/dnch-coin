ABOUT THE CONCEPT

A place to meet over good food

OLIVE & STONE is a fictional urban bistro concept created to showcase a restaurant website. The sample brand combines seasonal ingredients, relaxed dining and a fast digital menu. Guests can explore dishes and calculate a sample basket.

This story is illustrative and does not describe an operating company.
